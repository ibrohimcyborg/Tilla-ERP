4. Bugun 1:1 qilishning YAGONA ishonchli yo'li

1. To'g'ri ma'lumotli qurilmada: Cloud holati → ⬆ Bu qurilmanikini cloudga yuborish

cloudMajburanOl (8262) blobni to'liq oladi va ustidan yozadi — kassa ham, tartib ham, hammasi. Yuklashdan oldin avtomat backup ham oladi (8271).
