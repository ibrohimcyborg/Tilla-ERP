To’lov, offset va ishoralar

Vozvratda minus yo’q. Biz qarzdor bo’lsak — klientga +.

Sen aytding

«vozvratni oldidan minusni olib tashla keyin -10.00 emas, keyin +10.00 bo’lsa biz klientga «mana, +dasiz» deb tushuntiramiz»

Ikki ishora

1. VOZVRAT da minus yo’q. Blok nomining o’zi nima bo’layotganini aytadi — Butterfly Oddiy 12.00g, boshqa hech nima kerak emas.

2. Biz qarzdor bo’lsak — + bilan. -10.00g emas, +10.00g. Klient chekka qarab o’zi plusda ekanini ko’radi.

oddiy son -> klient qarzdor (bizda turgan ostatkasi) + bilan -> BIZ qarzdormiz "mana, +dasiz"

1 — oddiy: aralash to’lov + skidka + ostatkadan/berilgandan

Klient 16.09.2026 ================================================ OSTATKA Butterfly Oddiy 5.00g Diamond Oddiy 3.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 8.00g ================================================ VOZVRAT Butterfly Oddiy 2.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 2.00g ================================================ QOLDI Butterfly Oddiy 3.00g Diamond Oddiy 3.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 6.00g ================================================ BERILDI Diamond Oddiy 10.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 10.00g ================================================ QOLDI Butterfly Oddiy 3.00g Diamond Oddiy 13.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 16.00g ================================================ TO'LOV Diamond Oddiy 13.00g x 92.0 -1,196.00# berilganga 10.00g ostatkadan 3.00g Butterfly Oddiy 2.00g x 89.5 -179.00# ostatkadan 2.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 15.00g -1,375.00# Skidka -5.00# ================================================ TO'LOV USULI N 1,000.00# K 250.00# P 120.00# ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 1,370.00# ================================================ QOLGAN OSTATKA Butterfly Oddiy 1.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 1.00g ================================================

To’lov ikki manbadan. Diamond 13g to’landi: 10g berilganga (bugun olgani), oshgani 3g ostatkadan (eski qarzi). Butterflyda bugun hech narsa olmagan — hammasi ostatkadan.

1,196.00 + 179.00 = 1,375.00 jami 1,375.00 - 5.00 = 1,370.00 skidkadan keyin 1,000 + 250 + 120 = 1,370.00 N + K + P

Diamond 13 − 13 = 0 — oxirgi blokda yo’q. Butterfly 3 − 2 = 1.00g qoldi.

2 — klient plusda, offset hammasini yopdi

Klient 16.09.2026 ================================================ OSTATKA Butterfly Oddiy 5.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 5.00g ================================================ VOZVRAT Butterfly Oddiy 12.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 12.00g ================================================ QOLDI Butterfly Oddiy +7.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI +7.00g ================================================ BERILDI Diamond Oddiy 10.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 10.00g ================================================ QOLDI Butterfly Oddiy +7.00g Diamond Oddiy 10.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 3.00g ================================================ TO'LOV Diamond Oddiy 10.00g x 92.0 -920.00# berilganga 10.00g ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 10.00g -920.00# ================================================ TO'LOV USULI Offset Butterfly Oddiy 7.00g x 89.5 626.50# N 293.50# ------------------------------------------------ JAMI 920.00# ================================================

5g bor edi, 12g qaytardi → +7.00g — klient plusda, ya’ni biz qarzdormiz. Keyin unga Diamond 10g berdik.

To’lovda o’sha 7g Butterfly offset bo’lib pulga aylandi (7.00g × 89.5 = 626.50#), qolganini naqt to’ladi.

920.00 - 626.50 = 293.50 naqt

Butterfly plusi 7g — 7g offset = 0, Diamond 10 - 10 = 0. Hech narsa qolmadi, shuning uchun QOLGAN OSTATKA bloki umuman chiqmadi.

3 — offset yetdi, lekin klient hali plusda

Klient +10.00g edi, to’lovga faqat 5g kerak bo’ldi. Oxirida +5.00g turibdi — klient hali ham plusda, unga 5g qarzmiz.

Naqt umuman yo’q — TO'LOV USULI da faqat Offset.

Qarorlar — men shunday oldim

JAMI ham shu qoida bilan. 3-misolda +10.00 va 5.00 ning yakuni +5.00g — klient umumiy hisobda plusda.

BERILDI ham minussiz — u hech qachon minusli emas edi, vozvrat bilan bir xil shaklda qoldi.

TO'LOV dagi pul minusda qoldi — unga tegmadim, sen faqat vozvrat haqida ayting.

Maketda + yashil — ko’zga tashlansin uchun. Chekda rang yo’q, faqat + belgisi.
